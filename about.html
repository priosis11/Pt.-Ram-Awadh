About Our School

A UP Board school for Classes 1 to 12 in Masena Mirzapur, Ramnagar, Ambedkar Nagar.

Welcome to Pt. Ram Awadh Bal Vidya Mandir

For years, our school has served families of Ramnagar and nearby villages with quality education. Today we proudly teach 3000+ students with the help of 50+ qualified and experienced teachers and staff.

We offer the best classrooms, a big and safe campus, and a school bus facility so every child can learn with comfort. Many of our students have gone on to achieve great success in studies and careers.

Our Vision

To be a school where every child gains knowledge, good values and the confidence to succeed in life.

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable education with discipline, care and modern teaching methods for all students.
